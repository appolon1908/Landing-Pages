The name is a working brand, not a trademark or domain-clearance statement. The planned pilot is Santo Domingo. `https://breero.com` is the user-supplied public-domain reference used for planning links. Domain ownership and live operational readiness remain unverified; no purchase, domain change or launch is included. It is not a Git remote.

Preserve the supplied logo, favicon, service images and existing tokens. The legacy `--green` variable is an alias of the link token; it is not a separate green palette. The primary CTA uses orange with navy text. No new logo, certification badge, stock testimonial or invented partnership is authorized. Photographs of people or projects require an appropriate permission record; a fictional request illustration must be visibly labelled.

Spanish uses respectful **usted**, familiar Dominican service language and precise explanations. English is direct and calm. French uses international French and **vous**. Adapt the meaning; do not promise live capacity. Explain that a request is not a booking, an application is not activation, independent providers quote and perform work, and provider exclusivity concerns platform distribution rather than every contact the customer may make.

Avoid unsupported superiority, guaranteed leads/income/results, fabricated review counts, “verified” claims without a defined process, staffed-hours claims, immediate arrival and unapproved prices. “Free” requires an approved operational fact; proposed free customer requests must be clearly described as proposed. Negative disclosures such as “work is not guaranteed” are allowed. `_brand/banned-phrases.json` is an automated heuristic, supplemented by independent review.

Read `marketing/_brand/voice-and-cta.md` for localized planning CTA mappings. Page CTA and headline snapshots remain exact; the platform enum is a separate field. Do not force an eighty-character page headline into a forty-character ad field by truncating it.
