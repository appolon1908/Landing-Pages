Obtain appropriate messaging permission and honor opt-out requests from any channel. The service window opens and resets with each message **from the user**. ## After-hours and escalation

- ES: «En este momento no hay una persona disponible. No podemos prometer una hora de respuesta. Puede dejar su consulta para revisión o usar el canal de ayuda publicado. No envíe información sensible.»
- EN: “A person is not available at the moment. We cannot promise a response time. You may leave your question for review or use the published support channel. Do not send sensitive information.”
- FR: «Personne n’est disponible pour le moment. Aucun délai de réponse n’est promis. Vous pouvez laisser votre question pour examen ou utiliser le canal d’assistance publié. N’envoyez aucune information sensible.»

Before operation, the owner must supply an actually staffed escalation channel and hours. Do not display invented contact details. Human requests, unclear scope, complaints, accessibility needs, disputed fees or sensitive information stop automated questioning and enter the CRM's human queue. Do not collect identity documents, payment details or sensitive job descriptions in a marketing conversation. Tutoring requests involving a minor require a parent or guardian as contact.

## STOP and suppression

Treat STOP and ordinary requests to stop, including «no más mensajes», «cancelar», «arrêtez» and «désinscrire», as opt-outs. Do not require an exact keyword. Record the request and scope in the authoritative CRM suppression record before any future send; automation must not reactivate permission. Acknowledge in the user's language without marketing content. If the CRM or suppression check is unavailable, do not send. A request for service does not grant promotions permission. Re-engagement and seasonal drafts require an explicit, current promotions permission as well as an approved marketing template.

Notification drafts cover request receipt, missing provider details, application receipt, reviewed-application next steps, seasonal information and re-engagement. Utilities contain no promotional additions. Template approval is not permission to message a recipient. All examples use fictional sample values; no template has been submitted for approval.
